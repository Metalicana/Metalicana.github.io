I'm a PhD student at the University of Central Florida, advised by Dr. Yu Tian and Dr. Mubarak Shah. I work on long-horizon video generation and video diffusion models, and I also study AI agents in medicine.

My earlier research focused on image restoration, particularly image deblurring. Before joining UCF, I was a research assistant at the Cognitive Agents and Interaction Lab at the University of Dhaka.

Research

My research focuses on extending video diffusion models to generate longer videos while maintaining consistency over time. I'm particularly interested in how models retain and use past context as a video unfolds. Alongside this work, I study AI agents in medicine, including how they use tools and knowledge to support clinical tasks.

Keepsake: Selective Spatial Memory for Long-Horizon Video Generation

Abdul Mohaimen Al Radi, Kunyang Li, Yuzhang Shang, Mubarak Shah, Yu Tian

A training-free controller that continually reassesses which observations to keep in a fixed-capacity spatial memory. By preserving views with few substitutes, Keepsake improves long-horizon scene consistency across MemCam and WorldMem.

Aero-World: Action-Conditioned Aerial Video Generation from Inertial Controls

Abdul Mohaimen Al Radi, Kunyang Li, Yuzhang Shang, Mubarak Shah, Yu Tian

A controllable aerial video generation method that follows fine-grained inertial controls. Action tokens and a frozen physics probe improve motion alignment and visual quality on AeroBench.

Blind Image Deblurring with FFT-ReLU Sparsity Prior

Abdul Mohaimen Al Radi*, Prothito Shovon Majumder*, Md Mosaddek Khan

An FFT-ReLU sparsity prior for estimating blur kernels from a single image. The method works across a wide range of image types, with competitive restoration quality and up to twice as fast inference.
