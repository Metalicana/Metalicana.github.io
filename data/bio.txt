Abdul Mohaimen Al Radi is a PhD student at the University of Central Florida, advised by Dr. Yu Tian and Dr. Mubarak Shah. His research focuses on long-horizon video generation, video diffusion models, and spatial memory. He also studies AI agents in medicine. His earlier work focused on image restoration and image deblurring, including research at the Cognitive Agents and Interaction Lab at the University of Dhaka.
